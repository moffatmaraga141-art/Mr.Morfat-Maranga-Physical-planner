# Planning Projects

This section presents selected academic and practical planning projects
undertaken during my training in Urban and Regional Planning.

The projects demonstrate practical application of:

- Physical planning
- Spatial analysis
- GIS
- Land-use planning
- Neighbourhood planning
- Site planning
- Demographic analysis
- Field research
- Community-oriented planning

## Projects

### 1. Siana Ward LPLUD Plan
**Location:** Narok County, Kenya  
**Project Type:** Rural Planning

A Local Physical and Land Use Development Plan for Siana Ward,
supported by field research, household data, demographic analysis,
GIS and spatial planning.

[View Siana Ward Project](siana-ward-plan/README.md)
