01 — ABOUT

About Me

I am Morfat Maranga, a BSc Urban and Regional Planning student at Kenyatta University, developing practical experience in spatial planning, GIS, remote sensing, demographic analysis and community-oriented planning.

My interest is in understanding how people, land, infrastructure and the environment interact within places. I use spatial and demographic evidence to investigate planning problems and develop informed planning responses.

Through studio projects, field research and GIS-based analysis, I have worked on rural planning, neighbourhood planning, public-space planning and spatial analysis. I am particularly interested in using GIS and emerging technologies to support evidence-based planning decisions.

I also value continuous professional development. I actively seek opportunities to learn from practising planners, engage with the planning profession and strengthen both my technical and professional capabilities.

03 — SELECTED WORK

Planning Projects

Selected academic and applied planning work demonstrating how I approach planning problems through evidence, analysis and spatial responses.

RURAL PLANNING

Siana Ward LPLUD Plan

Siana Ward, Narok County

Planning Problem

Understanding the spatial, demographic and development characteristics of Siana Ward to inform a Local Physical and Land Use Development Plan.

Approach

The project combines field data, household questionnaires, demographic analysis, spatial analysis and planning considerations to understand the ward and inform planning proposals.

Key Evidence

  • Household data collected using KoboCollect
  • Population analysis
  • Population pyramids
  • Growth and doubling-time analysis
  • Dependency ratio analysis
  • GIS-based spatial analysis

Planning Response

Development of a Local Physical and Land Use Development Plan framework responding to the demographic, spatial and community characteristics of the ward.

Rural Planning GIS Demographics Field Research
NEIGHBOURHOOD PLANNING

Clayworks Neighborhood Plan

Biashara Ward

Planning Problem

Developing a neighbourhood-scale spatial framework for the Clayworks study area within Biashara Ward, responding to existing development, movement, environmental features and future land-use needs.

Approach

Existing conditions were examined through spatial analysis and aerial imagery before developing a neighbourhood masterplan, three-dimensional visualisation and street-level design proposals.

Key Evidence

  • Existing site analysis
  • Land-use considerations
  • Transport and accessibility
  • Environmental and wetland considerations
  • Neighbourhood structure
  • Street network analysis

Planning Response

A neighbourhood masterplan incorporating residential areas, commercial and mixed-use areas, education, health, light industry, agriculture, open space, environmental corridors and a transit-oriented node.

Neighbourhood Planning Masterplanning GIS 3D Visualisation
PUBLIC SPACE PLANNING

Dandora Wayleave Park

Dandora, Nairobi

Planning Problem

Exploring how an approximately 600-metre wayleave corridor could be reimagined as a safer, community-oriented public space rather than remaining underutilised or degraded.

Approach

Existing site conditions and community activities were examined to identify opportunities for recreation, public interaction, landscape improvement and supporting facilities.

Key Evidence

  • Site analysis
  • Existing community activities
  • Movement and accessibility
  • Open-space opportunities
  • Existing informal recreation

Planning Response

A proposed modern public park incorporating sports facilities, outdoor recreation, children's play areas, an amphitheatre, visitor spaces, street-art areas and supporting facilities.

Site Planning Public Space Recreation Urban Regeneration
